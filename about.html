ABOUT FIXIT SALONE

Making it easier to find the right person for the job.

FixIt Salone is a Sierra Leonean service marketplace designed to connect customers with local professionals in a simpler, more transparent and safer way.

OUR PURPOSE

Built for the way Sierra Leone works.

Finding a reliable plumber, electrician, cleaner, technician, mechanic or other skilled professional should not depend only on asking around or searching through scattered contacts.

FixIt Salone brings local service providers and customers together in one marketplace where people can discover services, view provider information, request work, receive quotes and keep a record of their jobs.

We are building the platform around Sierra Leone, with local regions, districts and pricing in SLE.

WHAT WE DO

One marketplace. Two sides.

🔎

For customers

Discover local providers, compare services and prices, submit requests, review quotes and manage your jobs.

🛠️

For providers

Create a professional profile, showcase your skills, set pricing, receive requests, send quotes and manage jobs.

🛡️

For trust

Build a record around accounts and jobs with verification infrastructure, reporting tools and administrative audit trails.

OUR VISION

A trusted digital home for local services.

Our vision is to become Sierra Leone's trusted digital marketplace for local services — helping customers find skilled professionals while giving service providers a better way to reach and grow their customer base.

Local firstDesigned around Sierra Leonean communities and businesses.
TransparencyClear service information, pricing options and job records.
