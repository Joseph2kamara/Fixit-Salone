TRUST & SAFETY

Built to protect customers and professionals

Verified identity, job records and reporting tools help FixIt respond when something goes wrong.

🔵 Identity verification

Users can verify their identity. Sensitive ID details stay private and are not shown on public profiles.

🧾 Job records

Requests, quotes, approvals and payment references can be tied to a specific job.

Customers and providers can report fraud, threats, harassment, damage or other safety concerns.
